1. INTRODUCTION & CORE CONCEPTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Znake is an artificial life simulation that couples the classic snake archetype
with genetic inheritance, local plant ecology, celestial rhythms, infinite signed
coordinate topology, and a faux-3D three-plane wireframe perspective engine.

Key Principles:
- Single Authoritative Simulation: Exactly one world simulation runs. The upper
  planes and wireframe perspective connectors are strictly visual projections;
  they do not spawn duplicate births, berries, collisions, or mutations.
- Deterministic Procedural Universe: Free Roam uses a 32-bit seeded PRNG to ensure
  that panned-away and revisited chunks generate identical vine placements and
  reference emoji lattices.
- Infinite Signed Space: Coordinates span all negative and positive integers
  without clipping, wrapping, or bounds errors.

2. MODES OF OPERATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
You can toggle between two primary simulation modes via the "00 / Free Roam & 3D"
menu or the top mode switch buttons:

1. ① FINITE ECOLOGY MODE:
   - Classic closed board bounded by `cols × rows`.
   - Toroidal wrapping enabled when walls are disabled; wall collisions fatal
     when walls are enabled.
   - Fixed camera viewing the full rectangular board.

2. ② FREE ROAM SANDBOX MODE:
   - Infinite unbounded 2D plane spanning negative and positive coordinates.
   - 64×64 spatial chunking with seamless Euclidean modulo `posMod(n, m)` and
     floor division `floorDiv(n, d)`.
   - Camera tracks the active selected snake head, automatically snapping to
     center screen.
   - Sliders `cols` and `rows` control the visible viewport window rather than
     world boundaries.
   - Deterministic coordinate reference emojis spawn at multiples of 3 and 5.

3. FAUX-3D THREE-PLANE WIREFRAME ENGINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Znake projects the 2D authoritative simulation into a 3-layer perspective sandwich:

- Layer 0 (Bottom Plane, L₀):
  The physical baseline simulation plane rendered at full opacity (α₀ = 1.0).
  Displays actual snake bodies, vine footprint tiles, berries, and death heatmaps.

- Layer 2 (Top Translucent Projection Plane, L₂):
  A scaled projection transformed relative to screen center anchor O:
      U(p) = O + s · [B(p) - O]
  where s ∈ [0.10, 1.00] is the top scale slider, and α₂ ∈ [0.00, 1.00] is top opacity.
  Optionally duplicates chosen visual categories (grid, snakes, berries, heads, emojis).

- Layer 1 (Middle Perspective Wireframe Connectors, L₁):
  Connects the bottom physical plane to the top projection plane:
      M(p) = ½ · [B(p) + U(p)]
  Connectors include viewport grid intersections and 4-corner perspective lines for
  every snake segment (including fractional subpixel child snakes).

Middle Wireframe Opacity Coupling Formula:
      α₁ = clamp[0, 1]( ½ · (α₀ + α₂) · (1 - s) · k )
Where:
  - When s = 1.00 (no height separation), α₁ collapses to 0.0 (no connectors).
  - When k = 0.00 (connectors disabled), α₁ collapses to 0.0.
  - At α₀ = 1.0, α₂ = 0.50, s = 0.60, k = 1.00: α₁ = exactly 0.300.
  The live computed value is displayed dynamically in the UI badge.

4. FREE ROAM SQUARE VINES & ECOLOGICAL FUSION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In Free Roam Sandbox, local food plants (vines) operate under square footprints:
- Standard Footprints: 1×1 (1 cell), 3×3 (9 cells), or 9×9 (81 cells).
- Monotonic Activation: Each chunk deterministically places candidate vine sites.
  Adjusting the density slider adds or removes vines monotonically via stable site
  hash thresholding.
- Square Triple-Vine Mutual Fusion:
  When three vines mutually cover each other's center points within their square
  influence footprints, they fuse into a single super-vine:
  * Retains the ID and lineage of the oldest vine.
  * Centroid position: arithmetic mean of parent centers.
  * Extent: Half-mean rule:
      targetSide = roundToOdd( max(1, (sideA + sideB + sideC) / 6 ) )
  * Genetic Traits: Averaged RGB color, foodGene, and moonSpeedGene.
  * Density Multiplier: Set to 2×, doubling continuous berry yield.

5. FIBONACCI GENETICS & REPRODUCTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Body Sizing: Founder snakes start at 1×1 grid cell size.
- Reproduction & Offspring: When a snake reaches maturity length, it reproduces.
  Child snakes inherit parents' colors with continuous drift mutations.
- Dependent Bond & Subpixel Division:
  Offspring can be born as Subpixel snakes (subdivisions 4, 6, or 9 per parent cell).
  While dependent, an active parental bond protects child tails from being stolen.
- Tail Stealing:
  Independent adult snakes with sufficient collision resistance can bite and steal
  tail segments from other snakes to rapidly acquire mass.
- Lunar Phase Rhythms:
  A continuous lunar clock shifts from New Moon to Full Moon, altering snake speed,
  berry nutritional yield, and clone probabilities.

6. CONTROLS & HOTKEYS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Desktop Controls:
- Arrow Keys or W / A / S / D: Steer the currently selected snake.
- Spacebar: Play / Pause simulation.
- Click Snake: Inspect snake genetics, lineage, and family tree.
- Click Canvas (Empty cell): Set food or place entities in Editor mode.
- Mouse Wheel: Zoom in / zoom out in Free Roam mode.
- Key T / Shift+T: Cycle visual themes.
- Key L / Shift+L: Cycle UI layouts.
- Key D: Toggle mobile on-screen virtual D-pad.

Mobile Touch Ergonomics:
- Virtual D-Pad: 4-way thumb touch disc for instant directional steering.
- Canvas Swipe: Quick drag gestures on canvas steer the active snake.
- Pinch-to-Zoom: Standard two-finger touch gestures scale the Free Roam camera.
- Bottom Sheet Drawer: Swipe up to inspect telemetry, adjust genetics, and toggle 3D.
